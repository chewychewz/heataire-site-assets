What every iX comes with

Inverter efficiency

The compressor adjusts its output instead of switching on and off. Sunlover rate it about 30% more efficient than a standard on/off unit.

Runs on your solar

PV Ready modes use surplus solar, or pre-heat the pool on a sunny day. Sunlover quote up to 40% lower running costs.

Rapid Heat

Faster heat-up when you want the pool ready, then it settles back to efficient running.

Control from your phone

WiFi as standard, and a 3.5 inch touch screen for temperature and schedules.

Lower impact refrigerant

R32, which has a lower global warming potential than older refrigerants.

Built to last outdoors

UV-resistant finish, a 5 year compressor warranty and 25 years on the heat exchanger.

How it uses your solar

Pair the heat pump with your solar system and it heats the pool from power you would otherwise export for very little. Choose Normal, Eco or Temp Plus to suit how you swim.

1

Sun comes up

It talks to a Smart Grid Ready inverter and picks up surplus power instead of sending it to the grid.

2

Rapid Heat

It switches to Rapid Heat and warms the pool quickly with that free power.

3

Heat stored

It pre-heats while the sun shines, so the pool stays warm when cloud rolls in.

4

Back to normal

At your set temperature it returns to normal running.
